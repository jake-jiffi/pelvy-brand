One colour

embroidery, foil, etching, a stamp

One colour is deep sage, or black on a mono printer, or white reversed out. The lighter shapes take the same ink.

Reversed: The white lines inside the symbol are gaps in the artwork, so they stay open when it is reversed. Below 48 pixels they close, which is what the simplified symbol is for.

No stroke below 0.25mm, which is where thread and etch stop holding a line.

Chart colour

a different job from the brand ramps

A brand palette is built for hierarchy and a chart palette is built for discrimination. Categorical colours here are checked to stay apart under protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia, which the brand ramps are not.

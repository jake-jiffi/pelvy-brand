What Pelvy doesn't do yet

It doesn't connect to clinical software. Clinics run the two side by side.

Symptom tracking and bladder, bowel and pad weight diaries come after the pilot.

It isn't TGA certified. Pelvy is education and support, and you stay in charge of the care.

Patients whose clinic doesn't subscribe can still use it, for $4.99 a month or $19.99 a year.

Breathe out

Literata for headings, DM Sans for everything you read twice. Nothing here will feel like a new brand to someone who knows the old one.

Reach, stretch, rest. One line weight, one colour, no faces, the same raised arm as the logo.

Signature: the one-line figure. It replaces the stock illustrated people on the site with drawings nobody else has.

The tradeoff: this is the closest to what exists, so it changes the least, and soft sage with rounded corners is where every wellness app already lives. A hospital may read it as a consumer app.

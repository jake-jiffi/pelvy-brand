Pelvy brand, territory round, 12 October 2026

Three directions for a modernised Pelvy

Where the brand is today

The logo exists only as a 228 pixel image. The website, the app and the store listing use three different greens and two type systems. White text on the site's sage buttons measures 2.97 to 1, and the accessibility standard asks for 4.5.

A. Clinic-grade

Health infrastructure. One deep green, one typeface drawn for legibility, the count ring as the repeated device.

B. The good physio

The kind clinician in app form. Today's sage and serif headings, redrawn, with one-line figure drawings.

C. The count

Built on counting. Oversized numerals, hairline rules, one grotesque and almost no colour.

Which way should Pelvy travel?

All three keep the name, a green from the same family and the figure that makes a P. They differ in who they reassure first: a health service, a patient at home, or someone who has seen every other pelvic health app.

Chosen: B, The good physio

Jake picked B on 12 October 2026. One change came with it: the one-line figures are dropped, because a drawing cannot show a pelvic floor exercise and would only decorate. The signature is now the clinician's own note and the count. The identity, voice and proof are built on B, and A and C stay here for the record.

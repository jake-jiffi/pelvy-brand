For General Users

How Can Pelvy Help You?

Pelvy is the only pelvic health app designed to support anyone experiencing pelvic floor, bladder, bowel, pain, or sexual dysfunction.

Every feature has been shaped by feedback from real patients and health professionals.

If you subscribe and decide Pelvy isn’t right for you, you can cancel within seven days for a full refund — no questions asked. We’re not interested in adding to your financial burden of healthcare if it’s not the right timing for you.

Why use Pelvy over other Pelvic Health Apps?

Pelvy brings everything together in one secure place – combining education, pelvic floor exercises, mobility programs, and reminders, all created by a qualified pelvic health physiotherapist.

 

During our pilot phase you’ll receive:

  • Affordable subscription pricing. $99 for 1-year. That’s less than half of your typical initial appointment.

  • Free access to our 8 week Pelvic Health 101 course for your area of interest and goals. This could be for strengthening, bladder urgency, bowel health, and pelvic pain.

  • Personal onboarding with Amelia, Pelvy’s founder and pelvic health physiotherapist

  • Optional community support groups.

  • Discounts to pelvic health products for Pelvy users.

Pelvy is ad-free, evidence-based, and focused solely on helping you improve your pelvic health. 

Our Features at a Glance:

  • Custom pelvic floor exercise programs – tailored to your symptoms, ability, and goals.

  • Trusted education – learn what can help you and support healthy bladder, bowel, and sexual function.

  • Symptom tracking (coming soon) – track pelvic health, bladder, bowel, sexual, prolapse and pain symptoms to identify what helps or worsens them.

  • Information storage – keep important advice handy from your clinician. 

  • Find a clinician – find a local pelvic health clinician with the right qualifications and experience.

  • Unlimited reminders – stay on top of your exercises with discreet and unlimited push notificatons.

Why would I use an app to help improve my pelvic health?

Managing pelvic health can be complex. Between remembering exercises, following your clinician’s advice, and finding trustworthy information, it’s easy to feel overwhelmed. Pelvy was designed by pelvic health physiotherapists to bring the essentials together in one secure app.

During our pilot phase, Pelvy includes:

  • Education sharing: Your clinician can send you personalised notes and evidence-based resources, so you always have accurate information in one place.

  • Pelvic floor exercises: Guided programs for connection, coordination, relaxation, and strengthening, tailored to common pelvic health needs.

  • Reminders: Simple prompts to help you stay consistent with your exercises and appointments.

As Pelvy grows, new features will include general mobility and strength programs, a clinician directory, and calendar tools to link symptoms, habits, and progress.

Pelvy makes it easier to stay informed, supported, and consistent, so you can focus on improving your pelvic health with confidence.

I don't have a clinician, can I still use Pelvy?

Not yet – but soon.

Pelvy is currently available only through participating clinicians as part of our early pilot program. We’re building towards individual access, but this requires additional infrastructure, compliance steps, and investment to ensure we can support users safely and sustainably.

Our goal is to make Pelvy accessible to anyone who wants to learn more about their pelvic health, even without direct clinician input. As part of the next stage, Amelia (Pelvy’s founder and physiotherapist) will personally onboard a small group of interested individual users, offering guidance and education based on your symptoms and goals.

If you’d like to be part of that early group, you can join the waitlist and we’ll contact you when individual onboarding becomes available.

Why would I want to strengthen my pelvic floor?

Your pelvic floor can be made weaker over time by the following factors:

—If you do not activate your pelvic floor when it is under high load (frequent heavy lifting, high impact exercise, straining on the toilet, repeated coughing or vomiting, being overweight is a heavy load as well.)
—If you are pregnant – the weight of a baby is a significant load on the pelvic floor
—If you’ve given birth vaginally as your pelvic floor has stretched
—Ageing


Even if you do not have symptoms of weakness, if you experience any of the above factors or have prolapse you are likely to benefit from learning how to use your pelvic floor properly and with a pelvic floor strengthening program. It is also ideal to be preventative – so if you are exposed to high loads you should learn how to use your pelvic floor and ideally, strengthen it. The gold standard is to see a pelvic health physiotherapist as they will assess and prescribe a program that is tailored to your current function and needs. If you do not have a pelvic health physiotherapist, Pelvy can help you to come up with your own pelvic floor program. If you do not notice any change and improvement to your symptoms or if you have any pain or worsening of symptoms with pelvic floor exercises we urge you to see a pelvic health physiotherapist as you may not be doing them correctly or pelvic floor exercises might not be suitable and they will know what exercises you should be doing instead.

Why would I want to relax or 'down-train' my pelvic floor?

For a range of reasons, some people can have a pelvic floor that is too toned, tight, or that struggles to relax. If you experience any pelvic pain – pelvic floor relaxation or down-training should be in your tool box. Sometimes urinary leaking can be a result of toned, tight, or overactive pelvic floor muscles. Pelvy has an extensive education section to help you decide if you could benefit from pelvic floor relaxation or downtraining exercises. Once you make this decision we have all the exercises you need to help relax or down-train your pelvic floor. You will also be able to do any of the most commonly prescribed and effective stretches and mobility exercises that pelvic health physiotherapists prescribe to their patients.

Why would I track my symptoms?

We apologise but our symptom tracker isn’t part of the pilot or beta. We have a prototype ready if you’re keen to have a look, and hope to develop these features in 2026.

In the future, we plan to have full symptom tracking for all bodies for if you wish to log and monitor any pelvic health symptoms, including bladder, bowel, sexual, prolapse, reproductive health, and pain symptoms. You will be able to track your activity (like exercise, having a physiotherapy appointment, taking medications, and more) so that you can build an awareness of how certain activities positively or negatively impact your symptoms or how your symptoms vary over time.

We want you to be able to easily see how your symptoms change over time by generating a report. Gone are the days of having to write down everything in the notes section on your phone or in a paper diary. Our easy-to-use, comprehensive symptom-tracking feature saves you time and hassle by making tracking symptoms easy.

We envisioned our symptom tracking reports to help with:

—You should become aware of what impacts your symptoms so that you can then make the changes needed to improve your pelvic health condition.

—Your clinician will better and more efficiently understand how all your different symptoms are related, saving you precious appointment time.

What is a bladder or bowel diary and why would I complete one?

We apologise but our bladder or bowel diaries isn’t part of the pilot or beta. We have a prototype ready if you’re keen to have a look, and hope to develop these features in 2026.

Depending on the condition, a bladder or bowel diary is usually requested by your pelvic health clinician to help understand your symptoms or for diagnostic purposes. If you have an upcoming appointment with a pelvic health clinician for bladder or bowel related concerns, completing a bladder diary (if you suffer from bladder urgency or pain) or bowel diary (if you are seeking help with your bowels) in advance is extremely helpful and might save you from having an extra appointment. Ensure that you read the instructions, as your clinicians will need you to do the diary properly so they can properly analyse and interpret it.

You can also do a bladder or bowel diary on a semi-regular basis to track your progress/or lack of progress.

What is a pad weight diary and why would I complete one?

We won’t have the pad weight diary ready for the Pilot or Beta.

A pad weight diary is usually done over 24 hours and is done to get an idea of how much urinary leakage you are experiencing. Pelvic health professionals find this helpful and might ask you to do this semi-regularly (weekly, monthly) to see how your leakage is improving or worsening. If you do not have a pelvic health professional but want to know how your leakage is going over time, a pad-weight diary can be helpful. Be aware that your fluid intake and activity levels can significantly influence leakage, so please make sure to read the instructions prior to doing a pad-weight diary so that you are doing it accurately and make note of important information that can make your leakage better or worse.

How does Pelvy keep my information confidential and manage the cybersecurity of its products and services?

Pelvy takes your privacy and data security seriously.
We follow strict health data protection principles and use industry-standard technologies to keep your information safe.

Here’s how we protect your data:

  • Secure storage: All data is stored on trusted, encrypted servers (Google Cloud/Firebase) with protection both in transit and at rest.

  • Australian-based compliance: We comply with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and follow frameworks similar to international health data standards (HIPAA, ISO 27001).

  • Limited access: Only you and your clinician (if you have one) can view your profile. 

  • No third-party advertising: We never sell or share your information with advertisers or external companies.

  • Regular monitoring: Our systems are routinely checked for vulnerabilities, and updates are applied to maintain security.

If you’d like, you can also read our Privacy Policy for more detailed information.

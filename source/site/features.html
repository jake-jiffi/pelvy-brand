Features Our Patients Have Requested

Everything you need to support you through your pelvic health journey is now in one app:

Educational Resources

Educational Resources

Learn about normal pelvic floor, bladder, bowel, and sexual functions and how we pelvic health experts can help.

Our resources will guide you and ultimately you’ll learn to identify exactly when you’re not quite 100% and exactly how to get back to your normal function or who to speak to to help you do so.

Symptom Tracker

Symptom Tracker

Track and understand your bladder, bowel, pain, prolapse, and sexual health symptoms and more. Automatically generate a symptom summary for your pelvic health practitioner to see.

Exercise Programs

Exercise Programs

We make exercises easy by counting and guiding you through your entire exercise program.

Generate an individualised pelvic floor strength, coordination, and/or relaxation exercise program for your patient or as an individual.

Our exercise library has the most popular strengthening, stretching, mobility, and Pilates exercises prescribed by pelvic health physiotherapists – with options to tailor each exercise’s difficulty as required.

Find your pelvic health expert

Find your pelvic health expert

Access to local health practitioners through the ‘Find my health practitioner’ feature if you’re not improving in suitable time frames for your condition or desire more support.

Note Storage

Note Storage

A space to store the information provided by health professionals – enjoy no longer needing to hold onto or sift through paper handouts or emails anymore.

Unlimited Reminders

Unlimited Reminders

Never forget a thing with unlimited exercise, appointment, and medication reminders – customised to your preferences. You won’t miss a beat!

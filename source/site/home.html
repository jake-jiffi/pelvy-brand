Clinicians

Differentiate yourself from other professionals by subscribing to Pelvy and elevate your quality of service to your patients by giving them the tools they need to succeed.

Pelvy is available to all pelvic health clinicians and specialists. As a Pelvy subscriber, your patients can use Pelvy for free. Your patients will have access to our features which support them to keep track of and follow-through with the advice, tests, and exercises you prescribe.

Your clinical subscription comes with free advertising of your services and interest areas to our local clients and saves you time on administrative tasks!

General Users

Improving your pelvic health is often complex – we’re here to make the journey simple.

Pelvy has all the features you need to support you through your pelvic health journey.

We believe you should only require the help of ONE app to help you take charge of improving your pelvic health. We have pelvic floor exercises, general exercises, symptom tracking, education, a place to store your medical history or medications and more!

Features Our Patients Have Requested

Educational Resources

Learn about normal pelvic floor, bladder, bowel, and sexual functions and how we pelvic health experts can help.

Symptom Tracker

Track and understand your bladder, bowel, pain, prolapse, and sexual health symptoms and more. Automatically generate a symptom summary for your pelvic health practitioner to see.

Note Storage

A space to store the information provided by health professionals – enjoy no longer needing to hold onto or sift through paper handouts or emails anymore.

Unlimited Reminders

Never forget a thing with unlimited exercise, appointment, and medication reminders – customised to your preferences. You won’t miss a beat!

Exercise Programs

We make exercises easy by counting and guiding you through your entire exercise program.

Generate an individualised pelvic floor strength, coordination, and/or relaxation exercise program for your patient or as an individual.

Our exercise library has the most popular strengthening, stretching, mobility, and Pilates exercises prescribed by pelvic health physiotherapists – with options to tailor each exercise’s difficulty as required.

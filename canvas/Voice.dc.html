100 words (102 written)

Pelvy is a pelvic floor app built by Amelia, a pelvic health physiotherapist who works with pelvic pain. It counts you through programs for strength and for relaxation, breath by breath, with video for every stretch and reminders at the times you choose. When your clinic subscribes, your clinician adds their own programs and advice and you use Pelvy free. When it doesn't, Pelvy Lite gives you a physio-built starting program for $4.99 a month or $19.99 a year. Pelvy is education and support: it does not give individual medical advice, and it tells you when to see a pelvic health physio.

Said out loud

one breath

Most pelvic floor apps just tell you to squeeze. Plenty of people need to learn to let go first. Pelvy counts you through whichever program you need, breath by breath, and reminds you. If your physio subscribes, their program and their advice are in there too.
